## Proposed rules

Customers and users should not:

- Upload/share data without the required rights, organizational authority, and lawful permissions.
- Attempt cross-tenant access, impersonation, credential theft, unauthorized testing, privilege escalation or bypassing quotas/payment/authorization.
- Upload malicious workbooks, malware or exploit payloads; use resources for abusive traffic or disruptive automation.
- Submit secrets or another person's sensitive information to external AI without the required authorization and approved data-handling basis; use prompt injection to seek unauthorized records or hidden system data.
- Use the service for unlawful, infringing, deceptive, harassing or otherwise prohibited activity as defined by the approved policy.
